# 💼 Commercial IP Doctrine & Business Execution Strategy
**Idea Reference:** Parking Lot #15
**Author:** Fayzillo Ummatov

## 1. Commercial IP Protection Strategy
- Core AI orchestration engines, proprietary prompts, AST parsing logic, and custom training datasets are strictly isolated as protected Trade Secrets.
- Client deliverables (generated apps, business logic, frontends) are cleanly packaged and handed over with full ownership to the client.

## 2. Go-To-Market Offerings
1. **Autonomous Telegram Mini Apps (TMA) & Bots**:
   - Rapid 24-hour turnaround for automated business bots (HR applicant tracking, e-commerce, CRM).
2. **Full-Stack SaaS MVPs**:
   - Production-ready NestJS + Next.js backends delivered in days instead of months.
3. **Enterprise AI Agents & Autonomous Tooling**:
   - Custom CLI developer tools (`agy-tool` style) for development teams to save 80%+ on LLM token costs.
